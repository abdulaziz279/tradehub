Flexible Delivery Methods

Transparent shipping policies tailored for customer convenience and merchant safety.

Same Locality: Pay on Delivery

Deliveries made within the vendor's immediate municipality or city support Pay on Delivery (COD). Customers inspect items in person upon doorstep arrival before completing cash or Mobile Money transfers directly to the dispatch agent.

  • Same-day express dispatch available
  • Product inspection prior to payment
  • Direct vendor hotline coordination

Outside Locality: Pay Before Delivery

Regional, inter-state, or long-distance intercity parcels require Payment Before Delivery. Funds are processed securely through our platform escrow or Paystack integration prior to waybill courier booking.

  • Insured waybill & courier tracking pings
  • Guaranteed escrow payment protection
  • 24-48 hour regional delivery guarantee
